# NCM: Next-Concept Modeling (arXiv:2610.14162v1)

- **Paper:** https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14162v1
- **Code:** https://github.com/HazyResearch/ncm
- **Authors:** Hermann Kumbong, Ishan S. Khare, Iddah Mlauzi, Andrew Woen, Charles Ding, E. Kelly Buchanan, Simran Arora, Azalia Mirhoseini, Christopher Ré (Stanford / Together AI).
- **Date:** 09 Oct 2026 (cs.CL)
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-08
- **Status:** external research reference. Not a dependency, provider, or code path in `free-ai`.

## Summary

NCM is a two-stage, coarse-to-fine text generation framework:

1. **Multiscale residual VQ-VAE tokenizer:** encodes text into `K` levels of discrete codes, from short coarse levels to longer fine levels. Nested dropout and conditioning-code corruption are used during training.
2. **NCM transformer:** generates one level per step and predicts **all codes of that level in parallel**, conditioned on the cumulative reconstruction of previous levels. The tokenizer decoder maps the final reconstruction to text.

Generation therefore uses `K << L` sequential steps. Coarse levels process very few positions, reducing per-step cost. It also supports conditional generation with a prefix-LM attention pattern and test-time scaling via multiple candidates + MBR decoding.

## Reported results (roughly 100M-parameter generators)

- **LM1B:** better generative perplexity than the strongest diffusion baseline and ~21x faster.
- **OpenWebText:** competitive with the strongest diffusion baseline, ~10x faster.
- **XSum:** matches the strongest diffusion baseline's ROUGE-L, ~31x faster; NCM + MBR improves ROUGE within the same sampling-time budget.
- **WMT14 De-En:** 22.6 BLEU without guidance (11 NFE); 25.2 BLEU with MBR (132 NFE).
- **Sudoku:** 99.5 / 96.5 / 86.1% solved for easy / medium / hard with 12 NFE, above AR and diffusion baselines; tokenizer reconstruction is 100% exact on Sudoku.
- **Training budget:** ~4-5x fewer training tokens than strong diffusion baselines on LM1B/OpenWebText, including tokenizer training.
- **Scaling probe:** 97M / 317M / 725M generators improve LM1B generative perplexity at a fixed training-token budget.

## Limitations stated by the authors

- Fixed hierarchy depth `K` and per-level lengths; short continuations still traverse the full hierarchy.
- Tokenizer is trained separately (not end-to-end with the generator).
- Main experiments are ~100M parameters; scaling is limited to 725M on LM1B.
- Occasional artifacts at coarse-level transitions.
- Quality depends on tokenizer quality and the code-corruption recipe.
